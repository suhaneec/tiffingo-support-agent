TiffinGo Help Docs

TiffinGo is a made-up business. This document is practice data for a learning project.

TiffinGo is a made-up app that delivers home-style meals from partner home kitchens, as single orders or monthly tiffin plans.

Frequently asked questions

Q: What areas do you deliver to?
A: We deliver within 7 km of each partner kitchen. Enter your address in the app to see kitchens near you.

Q: What are your delivery hours?
A: Lunch orders 11 AM to 3 PM and dinner orders 7 PM to 11 PM, all days.

Q: Is there a minimum order value?
A: Yes, ₹150 per order.

Q: How much is the delivery fee?
A: ₹25 for orders under ₹300. Delivery is free above ₹300.

Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Usually 30 to 45 minutes. It can take longer in heavy rain or at peak hours.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Open Orders in the app to see the live status: Preparing, Out for delivery or Delivered.

Q: Which payment methods do you accept?
A: UPI, cards, wallets and cash on delivery.

Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: Yes, free within 2 minutes of placing it. Once the kitchen starts cooking, it cannot be cancelled.

Q: Can I schedule an order for later?
A: Yes, up to 2 days ahead, for lunch or dinner slots.

Q: Do you have a monthly tiffin plan?
A: Yes. Plans of 20 or 26 meals a month, for lunch or dinner, start at ₹2,400.

Q: Can I pause my tiffin plan?
A: Yes. Pause up to 5 days a month from the app, at least one day before.

Q: Is the food vegetarian?
A: Each kitchen is marked Veg or Non-veg. Veg kitchens cook only vegetarian food.

Q: Do you show allergen details?
A: Each dish lists common allergens like nuts, dairy and gluten. Kitchens cannot promise zero cross-contact.

Q: Can I ask for less spice?
A: Yes. Add a cooking note at checkout. Kitchens try to follow it but cannot guarantee it.

Q: My food arrived cold or spilled. What do I do?
A: Report it in the app within 1 hour of delivery with a photo. You get a refund or a free replacement.

Q: An item was missing from my order.
A: Report it within 1 hour from the order page. The amount for the missing item is refunded.

Q: How long do refunds take?
A: UPI and wallet refunds take up to 24 hours. Card refunds take 5 to 7 working days.

Q: How do coupons work?
A: Apply one coupon at checkout. Coupons cannot be used on tiffin plans.

Q: How do I change my phone number?
A: Go to Profile, tap Edit and verify the new number with an OTP.

Q: Can I list my home kitchen on TiffinGo?
A: Yes. Apply on the Partner page. Our team checks hygiene and taste before listing.

Policies

Policy: Cancellation and refund policy
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 2 minutes of placing them.
- Once the kitchen starts cooking, the order cannot be cancelled.
- Cold, spilled or missing food must be reported within 1 hour of delivery, with a photo.
- UPI and wallet refunds take up to 24 hours; card refunds take 5 to 7 working days.
- Accounts that misuse refunds again and again may lose the option to claim refunds.

Policy: Delivery policy
- Kitchens deliver within 7 km.
- Lunch slot: 11 AM to 3 PM. Dinner slot: 7 PM to 11 PM.
- Deliveries may be late in heavy rain or at peak hours.
- If the customer cannot be reached, the rider waits 7 minutes. The order is then marked as attempted and is not refunded.

Policy: Food safety and allergy policy
- Partner kitchens are checked for hygiene every 3 months.
- Every dish lists common allergens. Kitchens cannot promise zero cross-contact.
- Anyone who feels unwell after a meal should see a doctor and report it at once. Support passes it to the food safety team.
